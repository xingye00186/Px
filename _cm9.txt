Section 11 rewritten from "this session's lessons" into a scenario-indexed
catalogue whose ORDER IS THE DIAGNOSTIC PRIORITY — rule out the environment,
then suspect the harness, and only then attribute to code:

  11.1 Environment and toolchain, the first stop
       - GUI exe launch protocol: never Start-Process -WindowStyle Hidden; no
         window station means the Skia/Win32 backend blocks with CPU=0 and
         looks hung. This was once misdiagnosed as a machine-level lockscreen
         problem, and reversed twice.
       - Three-step SHAPE determination before any attribution: watchdog fired?
         CPU delta (spinning = infinite loop, ~0 = blocked at startup)? blocked
         at startup means look at build artifacts, not PHP.
       - Missing .build_hash makes the pipeline silently rebuild for tens of
         minutes, which presents as a hang.
       - exe argument protocol; a typo drops into the GUI branch and opens a
         window.
       - Build-artifact contamination spreads: cleaning one app is useless,
         build/ must be wiped entirely.
       - PowerShell/sandbox traps: $var eaten by php -r, no &&, no heredoc,
         github:443 blocked, mojibake in the terminal.
  11.2 The harness lies: four cases of a green/silent tool being wrong, plus
       the requires() defect from this session generalized.
  11.3 AOT-only: the five defect classes, native_types being a no-op under CLI
       so the whole family is invisible to the test suite, --skip-build, and
       minimal-reproduction as the true-positive standard.
  11.4 Cache staleness: key coverage must be >= what consumers read; four
       same-family bugs; inputs that do not affect the result must not
       participate in validity.
  11.5 Performance measurement: steady_fps only, cycles must match, pick recent
       baselines, triangulate, isolate directories, and choose the target by
       stage share. Plus distinguishing "cost newly exposed" from "cost newly
       added".
  11.6 CSS semantics, where concept confusion concentrates: unconditional
       used-value override keyed on display not tag, box-sizing being a no-op
       for width:auto, zero-box versus display:none, getBoundingClientRect
       always border-box, two wrongs cancelling out, and the VNode single-child
       object form.
